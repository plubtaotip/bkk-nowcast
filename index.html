Chapter 4 : Research Results
4.1 Prototype System and Parameter Configuration
Configured an 8-hour Lookback Window and a minimum Weight Factor of 0.4 to enable the system to respond rapidly to acute PM2.5 pollution episodes compared to standard EPA guidelines.
4.2 Accuracy Analysis
Evaluated via RMSE and MAE indices by comparing NowCast values against true 24-hour moving averages, demonstrating low error margins and validating the tuned algorithm's precision.
4.3 Volatility Reduction via Area-based Approach (Objective 3)
Demonstrated that area-based group forecasting significantly reduces standard deviation (SD) compared to individual station monitoring, effectively minimizing false alarms for the public.
4.4 Spatial and AQI Proportion Analysis
Utilized Convex Hull boundary mapping for 6 Bangkok zones and Donut charts to analyze air quality proportions comparing 24-hour averages against the NowCast model.
